## What it does

DPIA Assistant guides product and data teams through GDPR-style impact assessments: data mapping, necessity analysis, risk scoring, and mitigation tracking — producing a defensible DPIA document at the end.

## Key features

- **Guided workflow** — structured questions adapted to your processing activity.
- **Risk scoring** — likelihood × severity matrix with mitigation suggestions.
- **Data-flow mapping** — visual map of what data goes where.
- **Template library** — GDPR, UK GDPR, LGPD, and CPRA variants.
- **Sign-off workflow** — DPO review and approval trail built in.

## Who it's for

Product and privacy teams shipping features that touch personal data.
